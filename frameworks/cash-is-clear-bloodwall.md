# Cash is Clear® Bloodwall

**Function:** Diagnostic tool quantifying true financial drag.
**Structure:** Measures both real costs (direct cash outflows, fees, interest) and opportunity costs (lost revenue, delayed growth, operational friction) to reveal the full impact of cash mismanagement.
**Deployment:** Used during diagnostic phase to establish baseline urgency and quantify the cost of inaction.
**Verification:** Locked per Branded Asset Map v1.5.3. Canonical across all downstream expression.
