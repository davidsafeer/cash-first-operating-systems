# Cash is Clear® 13-Week Forecast

**Function:** Proprietary forecasting engine.
**Structure:** Integrated tracking for accounts receivable, accounts payable, recurring expenses, recurring revenue, ad hoc expenses, and linked credit card/savings accounts.
**Output:** Clear visibility of bank account balance trajectories, specifically identifying when balances go negative 13 weeks into the future.
**Extension Logic:** Standard 13-week horizon is extendable based on project lifecycle. Proven across 5-year continuous weekly forecasting for long-term project clients.
