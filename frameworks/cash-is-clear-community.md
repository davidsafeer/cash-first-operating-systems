# Cash is Clear® Community

**Function:** Tiered ecosystem wrapper for client and practitioner engagement.
**Structure:** Four tiers — Free (content consumers), Paying Clients (implementation), Advisors (certified practitioners), Advanced Advisors (complex case support with direct founder access).
**Deployment:** Built around access levels, implementation depth, and direct founder interaction. Operates on a freemium model where foundational education is public, but advanced diagnostic delivery and direct support are gated.
