# Cash is Clear® Maximizer

**Function:** Proprietary diagnostic tool.
**Structure:** Filters 240+ cash strategies across 20 categorical buckets.
**Selection Logic:** Driven by current business conditions, root-cause impact, and implementation timeline. Strategy selection is situational, not prescriptive. Owner control is one situational factor in the matrix, not a gatekeeper.
**Deployment:** Used to move clients from symptom-chasing to root-cause cash strategy. Replaces generic "240+ Strategy Diagnostic Tool".
**Delivery:** A live online tool advisors use to identify strategies and track implementation.
**Verification:** Locked per Branded Asset Map. Canonical across all downstream expression.
