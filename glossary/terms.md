# Glossary of Canonical Terms

- **Cash-First Operating Systems:** The category name. Forward-looking, fact-driven cash control, picking up where backward-looking accounting stops.
- **Cash-First Operating System (CF-OS):** The system a company installs.
- **Cash is Clear® 13-Week Forecast:** Weekly tracking model with an accuracy decay curve (extremely reliable 2 weeks out, a solid directional guide through week 10, best guess weeks 11–13).
- **Cash is Clear® Bloodwall:** Diagnostic tool quantifying real costs + opportunity costs to reveal true financial drag.
- **Cash is Clear® Maximizer:** Online diagnostic tool that pinpoints which of 240+ cash strategies across 20+ buckets fit current conditions, root-cause impact, and timeline.
- **Cash is Clear® Process:** Mindset → Measure → Model → Manage → Maximize. For business owners and businesses.
- **Cash is Clear® Process for Advisors:** Mindset → Mentor → Measure → Model → Manage → Maximize. For advisors learning the Cash is Clear® systems, processes and the CF-OS.
- **Cash Flow Conundrum:** Structural proof that informal systems become exponentially expensive as companies grow.
- **Soul Client:** SMB founders (revenue-positive, cash-negative) and small accounting firms seeking an advisory shift.
